## Why the copyright line names a person

Copyright in a work generally requires human authorship. Most jurisdictions do
not currently recognise an AI system as an author that can hold rights, and
purely machine-generated output may be unprotected altogether. Naming the
human who directed the work is therefore the accurate and the protective
choice: it makes the grant in `LICENSE` enforceable by someone, rather than
nominally owned by something that cannot enforce anything.

The agent's contribution is real and is credited in this file. It is not
represented as separate legal authorship, because that representation would not
survive scrutiny and would weaken the project's own claim.
